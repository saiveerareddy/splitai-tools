⛽ Fuel Cost Calculator

Work out the fuel cost for a drive or road trip, and each person's share.

Formula

Fuel needed = Distance ÷ Mileage. Fuel cost = Fuel needed × Price per litre. Highway mileage is usually 10–20% better than city mileage.

Enter today's fuel price in your city, since prices vary by state. Planning a group trip? Track all the shared costs with the Trip Expense Splitter.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for CNG or EV?

Yes. For CNG, enter mileage in km/kg and the price per kg. For an EV, enter km per kWh and the charging cost per kWh.

Should I add tolls?

Tolls are not included. Add them separately and split them too.
