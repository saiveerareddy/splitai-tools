Indian land measurement units

  • 1 square yard (gaj) = 9 sq ft
  • 1 cent = 435.6 sq ft = 1/100 acre
  • 1 guntha = 1,089 sq ft (mainly used in Telangana, Karnataka and Maharashtra)
  • 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft = 40 guntha = 100 cents
  • 1 hectare ≈ 2.471 acres

Units like bigha and biswa vary from state to state, so they are not included. Check your local land records for their exact value.
