🧾 Split Bill Calculator

Divide a restaurant, party or cab bill between friends, including tax and tip.

How to split a bill

  1. Enter the bill amount before tax.
  2. Add the tax or service charge percentage printed on the bill, if any.
  3. Add a tip percentage if you want to tip.
  4. Enter how many people are sharing. The per-person amount updates instantly.

Formula

Per person = (Bill + Bill × Tax% + Bill × Tip%) ÷ Number of people.

If different people paid different amounts (for example on a trip), use the Trip Expense Splitter. It tells you exactly who owes whom.

Frequently asked questions

Should tip be calculated before or after tax?

Most people calculate tip on the pre-tax bill amount, which is what this calculator does.

Can I split unequally?

This tool splits equally. For unequal payments, use the Trip Expense Splitter or the SplitAI app, which tracks who paid what.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. It is completely free, works on mobile, and needs no sign-up.
