🧮 GST Calculator

Add GST to a price, or work out the GST inside a GST-inclusive amount.

How GST is calculated

Adding GST: GST = Amount × Rate ÷ 100, and Total = Amount + GST.

Removing GST: Base price = Inclusive amount × 100 ÷ (100 + Rate), and GST = Inclusive amount − Base price.

Within a state, GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. For sales between states, the full amount is charged as IGST.

GST rates change from time to time, so always confirm the current rate for your product or service on the official GST portal.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST goes to the Centre and SGST to the State for sales within one state. IGST applies to sales between states.

How do I find GST from an MRP?

Choose 'Remove GST' and enter the MRP. The tool shows the base price and the GST included in it.
