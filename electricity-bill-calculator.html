How units are calculated

1 unit = 1 kWh. Units = Watts × Hours per day × Quantity × Days ÷ 1000. For example, a 1500 W AC running 6 hours a day for 30 days uses 270 units.

Real bills also include fixed charges, electricity duty and slab-based rates that differ by state. Enter your average rate per unit from a past bill for the best estimate.

Ways to cut your bill

  • Set the AC to 24–26°C and use a timer.
  • Switch to BLDC fans and LED bulbs.
  • Turn the geyser off after use.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the fridge set to 60 W for 24 hours?

A fridge compressor switches on and off. About 60 W averaged over the day is typical for a mid-size fridge. Check its star-rating label for exact yearly units.

How do I find my rate per unit?

Divide the total energy charges on a past bill by the units consumed.
