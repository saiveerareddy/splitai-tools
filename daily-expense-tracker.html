📒 Daily Expense Tracker

Log your spending in seconds each day. Everything stays private on your device.

Why track daily expenses?

Small daily spends like tea, snacks, cab rides and online orders add up quickly. Logging each one takes about five seconds and shows where your money really goes each month.

Tips

  • Add this page to your home screen: open your browser menu and tap "Add to Home screen".
  • Set a monthly budget to see how much is left.
  • Export to CSV any time to open your data in Excel or Google Sheets.

Only in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data will delete it, so export a CSV regularly.

Yes. It is designed for mobile. Add it to your home screen for quick daily use.
