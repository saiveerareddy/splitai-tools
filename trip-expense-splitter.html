✈️ Trip Expense Splitter

Enter what each person paid. You'll see who owes whom, using the fewest possible payments.

How it works

Add each person and the total amount they paid during the trip, such as hotel, fuel, food and tickets. The tool adds up all spending, divides it equally, and works out who owes money and who should receive it. It then suggests the fewest payments needed to settle everything.

Your list is saved on your device, so you can add expenses day by day during the trip.

Track trips automatically

For longer trips, flats or regular groups, SplitAI tracks every expense, supports unequal splits, and lets friends settle by UPI.

Frequently asked questions

How are payments minimised?

The tool matches the biggest debtor with the biggest creditor repeatedly. This settles the group in at most (people − 1) payments.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is stored only in your browser on this device.

Can I split unequally?

This tool splits equally. The free SplitAI app supports unequal and percentage splits.

SplitAI tracks group bills, who owes whom and UPI settle-ups. It's free.
