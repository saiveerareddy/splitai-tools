Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = X × Y ÷ 100
  • X as a % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100
  • % change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100

Common uses include exam marks percentage, salary hike percentage, price increases, discounts and profit margins.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate marks percentage?

Divide the marks you obtained by the total marks and multiply by 100. The second calculator does this for you.

How do I calculate a salary hike percentage?

Enter the old salary as X and the new salary as Y in the third calculator.
