🔤 Word Counter

Paste or type text to count words, characters, sentences and reading time.

Useful limits to know

  • Instagram caption: 2,200 characters
  • X (Twitter) post: 280 characters for standard accounts
  • Meta description for Google: roughly 150–160 characters

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute. Your text is never uploaded; it stays in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for Hindi, Telugu and other languages?

Yes. Words are counted by the spaces between them, which works for most Indian languages.
