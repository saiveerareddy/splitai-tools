📅 Days Between Dates

Count the days, weeks and working days between any two dates.

Common uses

  • Counting a notice period or leave days
  • Rent, subscription or loan period calculations
  • Project deadlines and countdowns

Working days here exclude Saturdays and Sundays. Public holidays are not excluded, because they vary by state and company.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include the end date?

If both the first and last days count (as with leave), tick 'Include the end date'.
